The tasks here follow the TCF format: writing in 60 minutes (3 tasks) and speaking in about 12 minutes (3 tasks).

The TEF is built differently, but most of the practice carries over:

  • Speaking, section A (a phone call about an advert, about 10 questions): practise with Speaking 2 · Role-play.
  • Speaking, section B (convince a friend about a document, answer their objections): practise with Speaking 3 · Opinion.
  • Writing, section B (a letter to a newspaper, at least 200 words, three arguments): practise with the opinion part of Writing 3, and write longer.
  • Writing, section A (continue a news story, at least 80 words) has no matching task here yet.
